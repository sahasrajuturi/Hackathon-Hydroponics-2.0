What is Hydroponics?

Simply put, hydroponics is the method of growing plants entirely without soil. Instead of stretching their roots to find nutrients in dirt, plants sit in clean water infused with natural minerals.

Why roots love it.

Because the roots are suspended directly in water or nutrient-rich mist, they never have to fight compact soil or droughts. This lets the plant spend all its energy growing massive leaves, crisp textures, and incredible flavors above the surface.
