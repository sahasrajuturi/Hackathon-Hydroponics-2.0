What is Hydroponics?

Hydroponics is a method of growing plants without soil, using mineral nutrient solutions in a water solvent instead. By delivering nutrients directly to the roots, plants grow up to 30-50% faster and use up to 90% less water than traditional soil farming. It is the core philosophy behind Farmspherica's automated ecosystems.
